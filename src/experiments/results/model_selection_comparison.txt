Reading:
  * clean data: all three selectors recover every system exactly.
  * greedy forward selection degrades from ~2% noise -- spurious velocity-quartic
    terms out-correlate the true cubics and it commits to them early.
  * the debiased LASSO path recovers the benchmark systems exactly further into noise.

The ~1-2% noise ceiling is a property of the greedy selector, not of
ordinary-least-squares Lagrangian recovery. Debiased LASSO is now the production
2nd-order selector (finding_L.main_streaming.DEFAULT_SELECTOR = 'lasso'); the greedy
path stays reachable via runDiscoveryStreaming(selector='greedy').
