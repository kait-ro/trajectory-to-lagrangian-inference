Reading (single seed per noise level, degree-4 library, both benchmark systems):
  * clean data: all three selectors recover the systems exactly.
  * greedy forward selection fails from ~2% noise (the spurious velocity-quartic
    terms out-correlate the true cubics and it commits to them early).
  * STLSQ -- starting from the full least-squares fit and thresholding down --
    recovers exactly at 2%, then starts keeping spurious terms at 5%.
  * the debiased LASSO path recovers BOTH systems exactly through 5% noise.

So the regularisation-path approach clearly beats greedy here, and the ~1-2%
ceiling in PROJECT.md problem A is a property of the *greedy selector*, not of
ordinary-least-squares Lagrangian recovery. The debiased LASSO path is a strong
replacement candidate. It is kept additive for now -- this is one seed per level
on two systems; a switch of the production default should first be validated over
more seeds, more systems, and the streaming (degree-expanding) path.
